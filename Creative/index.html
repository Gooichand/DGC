The Gallery: Selected Works and Case Studies

In the 3D gallery room you can walk through an exhibition of my most significant projects. Here is a detailed breakdown of work by Gopichand Dandimeni.

EVID-DGC — Blockchain Evidence Management

EVID-DGC is a digital forensic evidence management platform that anchors chain-of-custody records on the Polygon blockchain with immutable, tamper-proof records and storage on IPFS. It features an 8-role RBAC access model, dual MetaMask + email authentication, evidence upload, and auditable timeline tracking. Phase 3 is in progress: AI deepfake detection, metadata forensics and legal compliance. It challenged me to architect secure relational data, handle sensitive authentication, and make evidence provenance verifiable by anyone.

LOOK-DGC — Image Forensics Toolkit

LOOK-DGC is a digital image forensics suite with 50+ analysis tools. It detects JPEG ghosting, copy-move forgery, hidden edits and AI-generated artifacts, then produces PDF evidence reports. Built as a portable toolkit, it demonstrates my ability to turn forensic research into usable, fast tooling that runs anywhere.

evid-dgc-certificates — Verifiable Credentials

evid-dgc-certificates issues verifiable, tamper-proof credentials. Every certificate and badge is anchored on-chain and stored on IPFS, so anyone can verify a credential from a single link. It is a practical look at how blockchain can make trust auditable without a central authority.
